# 05 – Spectral signatures & vegetation indices

Report: [`spectral_indices.Rmd`](spectral_indices.Rmd) · [rendered HTML](https://toihr.github.io/Advanced-Remote-Sensing-Exercises/05-spectral-indices.html)

Sentinel-2 scene of Berlin (21 Aug 2020):
- digitized land-cover polygons (KML → GeoPackage) and their mean **spectral signatures**,
- 2-D **feature spaces** (green/red, red/NIR, NIR/SWIR),
- **NDVI** (incl. a simple water/land classification), **EVI** and **PVI** (soil line derived from the red/NIR feature space),
- pairwise comparison of the indices.
